Why forecast and pipe can diverge: For the current in-progress month, TimesFM blends booked-so-far, historical coverage-to-close conversion, and stage-weighted coverage across all forecast stages — not just the narrow Commit/ML pipe. That means the forecast can rise even as Commit/ML pipe falls, if deals are booking or slipping out of near-term pipe into later stages. For future months there's no in-month booking signal yet, so the forecast and the open pipe mostly move together — divergence there signals a bigger swing in the covariate mix (seats, NN/IB split, past-due, etc.) rather than booking activity.

Method detail

Target series: TEAM_SALES.SALES_SEGMENT_MRR where remapped_core_product_classification = 'CommerceHub', CHANGE_CATEGORY_GROUP = 'Rep Driven', mrr_source = 'PAST MRR', is_segment_mrr_rules__current_only = TRUE. Metric: SUM(DELTA_GCUR_MRR) by WATERFALL_DATE month — FX-adjusted USD net MRR with product splits applied.

Model: google/timesfm-3.0-pytorch (commerce_multivariate) with 20 channels: 4 past-only (deal count, NN share, trailing coverage conversion, forward_schedule_next3) + 16 past-future (all pipeline stage MRRs, attribution ratio, seat count, past-due MRR, NN/IB open MRR, in-month booked, booking-month secondary, FUTURE MRR waterfall secondary). Calibrated stage weights: Commit/Closed/Most Likely ×1.0, Best Case ×0.7604, Not Forecasted ×0.2851.

Holdout methodology: Origin May 2026, 3-month-ahead (Jun–Aug 2026) for commerce_multivariate. All holdout predictions were made before actuals were known. Per-month APE: Jun 26 25.0%, Jul 26 9.5%, Aug 26 30.1%.

Baselines: Not computed in this snapshot.

What this is not: TimesFM is an aggregate monthly overlay — not a deal-level rollup. Looker's forecast reflects current open-pipeline coverage × stage close rates (a snapshot of the funnel). TimesFM learns from 12 months of closed-won history plus how coverage has historically converted.
